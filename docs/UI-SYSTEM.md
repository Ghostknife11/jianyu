# Jianyu Android UI system

This document prevents product surfaces from drifting into separate visual languages. It is normative for the reference Android app; forks may replace it through `BrandConfig` while preserving behavioral and privacy contracts.

## 1. Product voice

- Speak to a caregiver in ordinary language. Put protocol names and implementation status behind progressive disclosure.
- The Today and routine family/settings pages address the caregiver as `你`; the 13–15 discovery composer deliberately hands the device and its decision to the child and addresses that child as `你`. Keep that voice through the result, decision confirmation, and post-choice acknowledgement; do not silently turn this handoff back into a caregiver decision.
- Keep the same audience in the composer's fail-closed hints and direct-call errors. A missing positive clue may invite 留白 or another sentence, but the 13–15 screen must not suddenly address the current holder as `孩子`; a missing AI connection belongs to the shared device and caregiver setup, not to the young person's personal key.
- In the 13–15 and 16+ Today selector, use `切换查看人` rather than calling the current holder a child. When AI is not connected during 13–15 hand-over, describe it as this shared device's missing connection and ask the family to set it up; do not imply the child owns the caregiver's API key. This is copy, not identity enforcement.
- Candidate sources shown in the 13–15 result must follow the same audience rule. The bundled offline demo uses audience-neutral descriptions across stages; the AI request explicitly asks for child-facing wording during 放权. These are presentation rules, never a claim that generated text can verify who holds the device.
- The 10–12 共选 AI request has its own audience: generated titles and explanations should be readable by child and caregiver together, start from the child's current pull, and keep practical checks and caregiver goals visibly separate. The local Gate and family choice still decide what is shown and selected; prompt wording does not prove model compliance or the child's agreement.
- Product surfaces use natural Chinese as the default UI language. Keep necessary proper nouns such as provider names and `AI`; label credentials `API 密钥`. Do not mix `BYOK`, `Context`, `Graduation`, `Local-first`, or protocol class names into ordinary headings.
- The per-call AI disclosure follows the same audience as its discovery form. Name eligible history `共享足迹`, excluded restricted history `不在共享足迹显示的记录`, and internal identifiers `家庭及成员编号`; do not switch to ambiguous `私密记录` or raw `ID`. The 13–15 disclosure addresses the child as `你`, including recent feedback and the World information boundary.
- User-facing lifecycle labels are 共玩、陪伴、共选、放权、成年交接. Internal schemas may continue to use the stable `GRADUATION` enum.
- Family-stage headings and rights labels must also make sense at 16+; do not keep calling a person in Graduation a child in that surface.
- Timeline correction controls and source labels also follow the subject's current lifecycle stage: childhood history may stay in the same event schema, but a 16+ person is addressed as `本人`. On a shared device, the correction dialog must not imply that tapping it verifies identity. Visibility labels describe what appears in the shared timeline, not a child-only decryption guarantee.
- Apply that 16+ vocabulary to choice status, child-authored signatures, member-role labels, and subject-specific operation records as well. Keep the historical event and author unchanged; `本人当时…` describes the older act without re-attributing it. In wrapping metadata rows, separate labels with layout spacing rather than a leading `·` that can land alone at the start of a line.
- On the Timeline, record signature and date occupy separate supporting lines at large text size; evidence date and sharing/source details follow that same rhythm. Hypothesis support/contradiction counts wrap as whole labels, not a long separator-joined sentence. At 16+, historical child-stream and child-expression labels use `本人` wording in the UI without rewriting the underlying schema or claiming that old content was newly authored.
- User-facing context labels are 孩子、学校、生活、世界. The protocol names Child / School / Life / World belong in developer documentation, not routine form labels.
- Lead with the next family decision, not a dashboard metric.
- In Settings, group AI and World under `机会来源`, and privacy, transport, recovery, and deletion under `资料与设备`. Do not repeat those cards in a status dashboard. Optional World setup remains collapsed until the family asks to connect it.
- In Settings, keep unfinished transport and recovery tools under one short `加密传输与恢复` disclosure by default. If a folder is already configured, show its status and controls immediately. Ordinary use should not look like an infrastructure-maintenance dashboard.
- A selected encrypted folder is `已选择文件夹`, not an already-running sync connection. Its developer-preview card describes the visible consequence of missing multi-device safety and old-copy cleanup in family language; implementation terms such as device signatures, enrollment, and compaction stay in developer documentation. The per-call transfer action remains explicit.
- If the device cannot read its saved encrypted folder settings, show a distinct retry state rather than an unselected-folder setup action. Preserve the old settings and explain that choosing a folder again does not recover the previous transfer key; keep the local Family Vault usable.
- Importing a portable recovery bundle is a two-step replacement, not a merge: first verify the file and masked recovery code without writing, then show the current and recovered household names and require a separate danger-colored confirmation. A wrong code keeps the input available for retry; cancel, leaving/recreating Settings, or selecting a new file clears the in-memory preview. If the local vault changed after preview, discard that preview and require a new check. A long household name must scroll inside the dialog rather than hide its decision buttons.
- Keep that selected-folder vocabulary in the parent summary and deletion confirmation too; `已设置` or `已连接` must not imply that a one-time folder choice already provides background or multi-device sync. In the unselected preview, describe the next action and current limitation in family language rather than calling it a client-encryption transport chain.
- An unconnected AI source shows its purpose, state, and a clear connection action; model IDs, keys, service terms, and custom endpoints appear only after opening that action. A connected source shows a compact status and secondary maintenance actions.
- Selecting the offline demonstration must immediately change the visible source status to `离线演示已选` and keep a reversible exit action. The demo is a distinct on-device mode, never a silent fallback or an AI result. Keep this source block compact inside the occasion composer so it does not outrank the child's current-interest input.
- When demo mode is selected, the Today page description must switch from formal AI disclosure/retention language to its stage-specific no-send/no-save preview boundary; switching back restores the formal description. The page title and child-facing audience stay stage-appropriate.
- In the 13–15 composer, demo consent names only the local preview and its no-send/no-save boundary; do not reuse the formal AI request's data-selection consent after the retention controls disappear.
- Offline demonstration has no durable author or record; hide the formal record-signature ribbon in demo mode for every lifecycle stage.
- Once the offline demonstration is selected, connecting AI becomes a secondary outlined action; do not visually outrank the pending demo-preview action with a filled setup button.
- The offline demonstration uses fixed templates with only a keyword substitution and local Gate checks. Explain that limit in the composer and result. Its input, source event, preview choice, veto, and 留白 must not be saved as family evidence, choice, or feedback; identifiable prior-version demo inputs, choices, and any dependent interpretations remain inspectable in a separate historical section but must not be presented as genuine child preferences or enter later AI context or intervention counts. Demo selection controls are explicitly labeled as previews and acknowledge without a vault write.
- Settings card titles name the source (`AI 发现`, `世界信息`), while buttons name the action (`连接`, `更换`, `断开`); a card must not keep saying “连接…” after it is already connected.
- Never describe a child as a score, level, type, ranking, or predicted outcome.
- Do not put counts of saved observations, family choices, or hypotheses in timeline section headings. A record's evidence provenance can show the support and contradiction needed to understand that specific hypothesis, but collection volume is not progress.
- On the shared Timeline, show actual choices, evidence, or interpretations before any empty state. Only show a whole-page empty state when no visible real content or restricted-record notice exists; missing choices must not bury existing evidence or imply hidden records do not exist. Expanded history controls do not need record counts.
- A visible saved choice may be deleted from its secondary record-actions disclosure after a separate danger confirmation. State that its linked choice and later-view records are removed while earlier interest evidence stays, and do not promise old encrypted copies disappear. At 13+, address the subject directly and require an explicit confirmation without describing the shared-device tap as identity verification. Keep delete and later-view actions unavailable while the vault write is pending.
- Shared empty-state cards center both title and body. The Timeline message uses two short, intentional lines at 360 dp / 1.3-font text rather than allowing `入口` or `主动` to split mid-word. It must not imply that an empty timeline needs filling, and the all-Graduation copy speaks about the person's own retention decision rather than waiting for a child's next interest. If audit events exist without saved choices or evidence, name the absence of choices/lines specifically and point to the operation record below; do not say the page has no records at all.
- Use the same verbs consistently: AI **discovers**, the local Gate **checks**, the family **chooses**, and the child **confirms / refuses / reflects**.
- Optional detail disclosures use the shared start-aligned text-and-chevron toggle with an explicit expanded/collapsed accessibility state. Across Today, Family, Timeline, and Settings, use `查看…` / `收起…` for the same action instead of mixing bare links and unrelated verbs. Keep labels short enough for 360 dp and larger text.
- The Today composer uses that same disclosure toggle for optional reality conditions, and Graduation uses it for household-copy decisions; neither optional section should introduce a competing full-width action style. A configured and unconfigured AI source use the same `选择离线演示` action label.
- Use `这次` for the current discovery and decision in ordinary UI copy. Reserve `今天` for an actual calendar-day fact and `本次` for technical or legal detail, rather than switching registers within one flow.
- Distinguish production capability, optional connection, offline demonstration, and development preview in visible copy.
- Distinguish a saved AI connection, an optional one-sample compatibility check, and a measured model recommendation. The reference app currently has no verified real-model quality grade.
- If the one-sample AI response reports an incomplete finish, name that status separately; do not call it a network failure, successful recommendation, or proof that a model is incompatible.
- A request that exceeds its response wait is also distinct from an incomplete finish, invalid JSON, or rejected credentials. Say the service response timed out, avoid claiming that no request was processed, and warn that retrying may incur another charge. A longer client wait is not proof that the model can produce usable opportunities.
- Saved AI or World service information is `连接信息已保存在本机`, not proof that either service responds. The optional AI fictional-sample check reports only that single response; Today and source-save notices must not promise a live connection before an approved request actually succeeds.
- Show a saved-source status once, then the next-action explanation; do not repeat the same status as both a pill and a paragraph on one card. Use the same short `开发预览` label and cool preview tone for unfinished transport, recovery, and export capability; warm decision tone is for a family choice or consent, not a feature's maturity.
- In a connected source card, keep the default explanation short: status, what the next action does, and its material cost or privacy boundary. Put extended protocol detail behind an explicit disclosure. Do not repeat the same safety caveat in consecutive paragraphs merely to fill the card; shortening copy must never hide a charge, external transfer, or unfinished capability.
- The saved-source card presents status, the next-action boundary, and maintenance controls first. Optional AI connection checking sits under `查看连接检查`; before its action, say plainly that only a fictional sample is sent and the provider may charge. A configured World source keeps the possible region transfer visible and places its longer data-boundary explanation under `查看信息边界`. Use `虚构样例` consistently in family-facing copy; the check is not a recommendation-quality grade.
- The fictional-sample check reports the number of doors left by the formal local Gate and Diversity Selector, not merely the number of parseable or Gate-eligible model candidates. If eligible candidates are all omitted from display, use a separate inconclusive state; do not call it a successful opportunity sample, a network failure, or a Gate rejection.
- AI and World source forms share `显示名称` and `保存连接` / `保存新连接`; a saved source uses `更换连接` and `断开`. A saved World service is `已设置` in the discovery preview, not `已连接`.
- World source copy may promise that separate child and family fields are not attached, but must not promise that a free-text region cannot contain a name or address; the entered region may be sent as written.
- The optional region field can reach both the approved AI request and a configured World-information service. Its input hint must not say it is for World information only. The per-call preview shows the full trimmed region that would be sent, without shortening away a potentially sensitive suffix; demo mode instead says no external request occurs while local constraints still filter fixed templates.
- In the saved AI source card, show that status separately from provider and model metadata; avoid a single long provider/model line that can strand a separator at large font scale.
- Use the configured brand name only where identity is the point: onboarding, the persistent app bar, and About. Ordinary flows name the real actor or mechanism—你、家庭、孩子、AI、本机、已连接的服务—instead of turning the product name into a speaking character.
- Safety, privacy, deletion, recovery, and capability-status copy is factual and brand-neutral. `BrandConfig` may replace identity and tone, but cannot rewrite these behavioral guarantees.
- Context Firewall excludes separate identity/location/key fields; it does not reliably detect names or addresses typed into free-text interest, goals, school/life context, region, or optional recent summaries. Per-call approval and the result receipt must distinguish those structural exclusions from user-written text and warn that the latter may be sent. Never say such details are `始终排除` without a verified text-level guarantee.
- A refusal, a plainly caregiver-led plan, or obvious external background without a separate positive child clue uses the same calm, ordinary language as 留白: explain that no new recommendation is requested and invite a child-originated clue if one exists. The shared hint must not falsely say the child refused when the input only states a caregiver plan or public background. Do not claim that the App perfectly understood the sentence.
- A count of recently selected options remains local by default. A selection does not prove an activity happened or the child liked it. If the family opts into recent-history context for this call, label the count separately in the AI disclosure and receipt; never silently send it or let it silently reject a new child-led request.

### Family-facing vocabulary

The app must not alternate among technical synonyms for the same thing:

| Meaning | Use in product UI | Avoid in ordinary UI |
|---|---|---|
| one actionable option | 入口 | candidate、Opportunity、方案、方向混用 |
| the set of different routes | 几扇门 / 这次看见的门 | 推荐列表、任务清单 |
| AI contribution | AI 负责发现 / 寻找入口 | AI 决定、AI 安排 |
| deterministic local policy | 本机检查 / 本机排除 | Gate、Policy |
| no action | 留白 / 这次什么都不做 | 放弃、错过 |
| stored family history | 家庭足迹 | 档案评分、画像 |
| encrypted local authority | 家庭保险箱 | Family Vault（普通页面） |
| public current information | 世界信息 | World Brief（普通页面） |
| 16+ lifecycle | 成年交接 | Graduation（普通页面） |

Provider and Pack ecosystems use stable machine IDs. UI localization maps them once through `UiLabels.kt`; screens must never display a raw ID or create a page-local alias table. Unknown ecosystem IDs display `其他入口`, and unknown source kinds display `其他来源` or `来源待确认` rather than leaking an extension code. The domain engine canonicalizes ecosystem aliases before diversity selection. Provenance must remain visible even when the source is not verified.

The `world-event` ecosystem marker is `世界线索`, not `正在发生的世界`: a future or unverified public item must not be described as an event already happening. `世界信息` remains the source label, and the separate verification state must stay visible.

## 2. Page hierarchy

- Main product pages use `JianyuPageHeader`: section label, one editorial title, then one plain-language description.
- Sections use `JianyuSectionHeader`. Do not invent page-local title sizes.
- Page and section titles explicitly use `onSurface`; they can sit directly in a screen list without a parent Surface, so relying on inherited text color makes them unreadable in dark mode.
- Card headings use `JianyuCardTitle` (`titleMedium`, semibold). Reserve larger headings for the page hero, lifecycle handoff, and individual opportunity titles.
- Opportunity and 留白 card titles use one shared `JianyuOpportunityTitle` with heading semantics and heading-aware line breaking; provider titles remain fully readable rather than being clipped or truncated at the narrow baseline.
- Shared page, section, and card titles carry Compose heading semantics; a title is not complete if it is only visually bold.
- The persistent app bar contains one compact brand mark and name. A page hero must not repeat the logo.
- A fork may supply a longer brand name: keep the app-bar name to one ellipsized line within its allotted width while the `本机加密保存` status remains visible. The full name still belongs in onboarding and About; do not truncate the BrandConfig value itself.
- Use `JianyuLayout` for the same 20 dp horizontal gutter, 18 dp vertical gutter, and 16 dp section rhythm on all four main pages; cards use the shared 16–18 dp padding and Material shapes.
- All four main pages use `JianyuPageHeader`. The Today title changes with discovery, result, and Graduation state, but does not invent a separate page-title style inside a colored card.
- In the Today form, keep the child's one-sentence entry visible before optional machinery. Start its text field at one line and let it grow with the draft, rather than reserving an empty multi-line box on every first visit. Four-stream context details belong in the expandable preview; the current source status and its next action precede the optional `语音转文字` text action. Voice is not a competing full-width button or the first action above the child's input. At 360 dp / 1.3 font, the unconfigured AI action should remain wholly visible in the initial 4–15 views; the teen's shared-device and no-send/no-save explanation remains visible even when that leaves a smaller gap above navigation.
- The one-sentence field starts with a human hint, not a `0/800` meter. Show a character count only near the 800-character input limit; this is an input boundary, never a family progress score.
- Routine forms and record attribution use neutral cards. Green is for the active discovery/lifecycle moment, not an entire technical configuration form. Warm cards are for consent or an actual family decision, not an optional-details toggle.
- Onboarding's AI-discovery promise is a discovery moment, not a family decision; use the shared discovery tone and shared card-heading component there as well.
- Onboarding shows the full mission, a short explanation, and the primary `开始建立家庭资料` action before its optional promise cards; a family must not scroll through every explainer just to begin. The data-entry step uses the shared page-header hierarchy and starts at its heading, never at the previous step's scroll position. Its copy names the data actually collected and the local-storage boundary.
- The local-only sharing cost is visible in ordinary App copy, not only README: two caregivers must use the same device to view the same household until real multi-device sync exists. A manual recovery bundle is a transfer/replacement tool, not automatic merge. Settings may describe LAN/NAS/WebDAV/S3 as intended modes only while plainly saying they are not yet available as automatic multi-device sync.
- An unreadable existing Family Vault, or a leftover `family.vault.pending` from an interrupted write, is not an empty household. Startup shows a separate fail-closed page with `重试打开` and no new-family action, so potentially recoverable ciphertext cannot be overwritten through onboarding or a later save. The page does not silently discard or promote a pending file; a dedicated recovery flow remains future work. During the first local save, onboarding disables fields/back/duplicate submission and shows `正在保存…`. An ordinary failed write keeps the draft and offers retry on the same form; a write that leaves an unresolved pending file instead closes the form and fails safe.
- Onboarding keeps the brand name as its sole display headline, the tagline as the secondary title, and the full mission as readable body emphasis rather than a competing second headline. Its first-run copy describes future per-request data choice without implying consent or disclosure before setup.
- The setup form's primary action names the immediate local write (`保存家庭资料`), not a successful opportunity search: formal AI discovery still needs a configured service and fresh per-request approval.
- In the formal discovery composer, show the current record author and shared-device identity caveat before retention choices, Provider disclosure, final consent, and the primary discover action. Keep the ribbon absent from the no-save offline demonstration.

## 3. Color semantics

- Green `primaryContainer`: a small discovery/stage/selected-state marker, or a safe ready state. A routine form or full child card stays on the neutral surface even when active.
- Graduation uses a compact green stage marker, two neutral decisions (personal export and household-copy retention), and a cool preview notice for the experimental export. Do not paint the entire hand-off surface as an opportunity-discovery card.
- Warm `secondaryContainer`: a human decision, consent reminder, authorship, or a gentle caution.
- Neutral surface containers: history, settings, forms, metadata, and empty states.
- Cool slate tertiary colors mark development previews; red error colors mark destructive or high-risk actions. They must remain visibly distinct in both light and dark themes.
- Destructive actions use shared danger button variants on the card, in the options panel, and in the confirmation dialog. A red danger card with a green default button sends contradictory signals; use the error-colored button components instead.
- Never rely on Material defaults for container colors. Both light and dark `surfaceContainer*` tokens are explicitly defined in `Theme.kt`.
- New cards use `JianyuCard` and one of five semantic tones: neutral, discovery, human decision, development preview, or danger. Avoid page-local corner radii and one-off container colors.
- Information nested inside a card uses `JianyuInset` with the same semantic tones. Product screens do not create one-off tinted `Surface` blocks or translucent colors for these explanations.
- Semantic mapping is fixed across pages: discovery and active lifecycle markers use green; consent and family decisions use warm yellow; ordinary data, forms, and settings use neutral surfaces; unfinished preview capability uses cool slate; destructive actions use red.
- Only primary opportunity cards may request the shared 2 dp emphasis elevation. Semantic color still comes from the same component; screens must not recreate elevated cards locally.

## 4. Controls

- Generic selected states use `JianyuChoiceChip` and the primary green container. Warm yellow remains reserved for cards that represent a human decision, consent, authorship, or gentle caution.
- On the multi-child Family page, every stage card stays neutral and only the currently viewed child gets a green `当前查看` status pill. Every card states the current decision authority; the full five-stage guide and child-rights explanation expand for one child at a time. Unfinished privacy or identity boundaries remain visible even when that guide is collapsed.
- Keep Family and populated Timeline introductory copy short enough that common words such as `结论` and `打卡` do not break mid-word at 360 dp / 1.3 font scale. Prefer direct language about whose view is recorded and the absence of scoring over explanatory paragraphs in the hero.
- Stage authority and rights use a full-width label-over-explanation block, not a fixed leading label beside a weighted paragraph; the decision sentence must remain readable at narrow widths and large font scales.
- The Family page leads with children and their current decision authority before caregiver record attribution. The recorder switch still stays available on the same page and must plainly say that it is a signature choice, not authentication.
- Bottom navigation follows the same selected-state language: green icon and label on a green container. It must not inherit a second accent color from Material defaults.
- Read-only metadata and status capsules use `JianyuPill` / `JianyuTextPill`. Their neutral, discovery, human-decision, active, and danger tones share one shape, spacing scale, and text hierarchy; screens must not recreate 99 dp rounded `Surface` blocks.
- Dynamic member and child selectors wrap to additional rows; never assume a household has only one or two people or that names are short.
- On the occasion-first Today page, multiple children must not occupy the entire first screen as an always-expanded chip list. Show the current child beside an explicit `切换孩子` control; expand the full wrapping selector on request and collapse it after a selection. The Family page continues to show every member and stage.
- One filled button per decision group is the primary action.
- Family forms use the same noun and verb from the entry action through the form heading and confirmation. While a family form is writing locally, its action says `正在保存…`; other family-form actions pause. Close and clear a draft only after the vault write succeeds. On failure, keep the draft visible for correction or retry and show the error through the shared feedback surface.
- The Family-page recorder selection persists for later new records, so label it `新记录由谁署名`; `这次由谁记录` belongs only to the current discovery composer. Shared Timeline headings and audit labels describe choices, refusal, and 留白 without assuming a caregiver made them. Keep the actual record signature visible without representing shared-device switching as identity verification.
- The same save-state rule applies to AI and World-information connections: an accepted tap is not a saved connection. Keep the masked credential draft and form visible while the encrypted local settings write is pending or fails; show a failed local save persistently inside that source's form using the shared warm inset, rather than relying only on a transient snackbar. Clear the credential input only after success or explicit cancellation. Do not allow a second source-form action or navigation during the pending write.
- A saved connection does not need a new primary action: its optional sample check and replacement are secondary, and disconnect is a low-priority text action. Use a filled action when creating or saving a connection.
- Saved AI and World sources share the `JianyuSavedConnection` status/service hierarchy. Today uses its compact status-and-service form without a model-ID row; the exact model remains visible in Settings. Secondary source explanations use `JianyuSupportingText` so one page does not silently turn metadata into a competing headline. Keep the important per-call sending and possible-charge notices visible, but avoid repeating the same saved-versus-live status in multiple paragraphs.
- Saved service and model names may be longer than the card width. Wrap them as readable heading-like phrases while leaving the exact names visible; give an unusually long model name its own `模型` label instead of stranding `模型：` alone. Never shorten the service identity or hide `更换连接` / `断开` merely to make a screenshot tidy.
- In Settings, AI and World-information connections use one shared `更换连接` outlined action and `断开` text action. The optional AI fictional-sample check stays in its own expandable section, never in the same wrapping row where it can strand `断开` alone. The optional World-information entry action names what it connects (`连接世界信息服务`), rather than the ambiguous `连接可选服务`.
- `断开` for a saved AI or World-information source opens a separate danger-styled confirmation before removing device-local connection details and any stored API key. Its short lines distinguish that removal from the unaffected Family Vault, and explain that already sent provider data or queries cannot be recalled. Cancel keeps the connection; confirming may require entering the credentials again later.
- The unconfigured AI card in Settings uses the same filled `连接 AI 服务` action as Today; both lead to the same setup flow. The optional World-information connection remains secondary.
- The unconfigured AI status says `AI 服务尚未设置` on Today and `尚未设置 AI 服务` in Settings. A saved Today source shows provider and model on separate lines rather than joining them with a fragile separator.
- Outlined buttons open a secondary flow or optional details; text buttons reveal low-priority details, switch modes, cancel, or defer.
- Call school facts `学校记录` / `学校情况`, family availability `家庭安排`, and credentials `API 密钥` in product UI. `schoolWindow`, `lifeContext`, and `apiKey` remain stable internal names.
- Dates use a calendar control and display `yyyy年M月d日`; raw year/number text fields are not acceptable for birthdays. The calendar trigger is an input field beside other form fields, so its outline uses the same small-corner field shape rather than the pill shape reserved for actions.
- A retained/imported member younger than 4 keeps a calm, read-only Today state: explain that discovery starts at age 4, with no composer or discovery result actions. The Family card names this as not yet in an applicable stage; never label a 3-year-old `共玩`. Do not imply that the family must prepare a task before that birthday.
- Both birth and assessment calendar dialogs must follow the Chinese product language and the light/dark accent tokens even on an English-language device. The Android framework picker needs a localized context plus a scoped default-locale switch while the modal is open for its large date header and lazily generated accessibility dates; restore the prior process locale when it closes, including on error. Keep an isolated UI regression test for this boundary and visually check the dialog at 360 dp / 1.3 font in both themes.
- Calendar-only facts such as birthdays and assessments never display an invented `00:00`. Actual instants may add local `HH:mm` after the same Chinese date format.
- School assessment entry reuses the domain validator before enabling save once required fields are present. Invalid score ranges, dates, or optional comparisons show the same plain-language reason beside the action, rather than looking ready and failing only after a tap.
- Inputs explain why the data is needed and whether it stays local, is saved, or may be sent to a Provider.
- In 13–15 hand-over, show retention and any Provider disclosure before the child's final consent. Child consent and Provider approval are consumed by one request; a new attempt or a material change to the prompt, constraints, retention, source, or shared-history scope must require fresh confirmation. Recent-history inclusion defaults off for each new attempt.
- A formal AI request shows that the family's chosen provider may charge at the per-call consent boundary, in the same neutral factual voice as the data-sending warning. The optional fictional-sample check repeats its own possible charge before the check button.
- The hand-over composer must not offer a caregiver recorder selector while saved expressions are authored as the child. If the linked child member is missing, saving fails closed instead of silently substituting a caregiver signature; the shared-device identity limitation remains visible.
- The same authorship rule continues through the child's choice and optional feedback in the immediate hand-over flow. Timeline caregiver feedback retains caregiver authorship; do not display a child-facing decision while silently writing its audit event as a caregiver. Never rewrite old event authors to guess who held a shared device.
- In 10–12 共选, choices and later caregiver-relayed child views are marked shared-with-child but keep the caregiver as author. The acknowledgement and Timeline must not translate that visibility into a claim that the child personally tapped, agreed, or participated; the optional view keeps its explicit `代记` wording.
- After discovery, show the provider mode and local-retention outcome immediately, but keep the complete included/excluded data receipt behind a clearly named disclosure control. Privacy must stay inspectable without turning every result header into a protocol dump.
- Keep that compact receipt short enough for the first opportunity heading to enter the 360 dp / 1.3-font first view when one valid entrance exists. Name the three roles plainly (`AI 找入口，本机筛选；你/你们来选`) and keep the external service's terms and local retention outcome visible; the expanded receipt still carries exact category details.
- Result count belongs in the page description, not a second headline. Put mode and retention in one secondary, neutral receipt; offer “换个线索” as a small action, then show the route overview and the entry cards.
- When the result count refers only to actionable opportunities, phrase 留白 as an additional choice; never imply it is already included in that count.
- An empty result is not proof that 留白 is the best choice. If any source failed, explicitly say the search was incomplete and offer retry or 留白. If completed sources offered only Gate-eligible caregiver-primary routes, explain that these were not presented as the child's entrance; do not call them Gate rejections or infer that the child lacks interest. Otherwise say only that no entrance is available to show this time.
- If an otherwise completed AI request yields only a time-sensitive named activity without an original source, explain in the empty-result lead that the App withheld that activity locally. Keep the family free to change the clue or choose 留白; do not show the invented event as a usable door or call the model's claim verified.
- Show the route overview only when at least two actionable entrances need comparison. With one entrance, show its full card directly instead of repeating a potentially long generated title above it; 留白 remains one full decision card. With zero entrances, the page description carries the uncertainty and next step, any source failure gets its own caution card, and 留白 remains one full decision card; do not add a second overview card containing only the same 留白 title or call it `另一扇门` when there is no first door.
- When a source failed, `调整后再试` returns to the composer with the unsent local input draft intact, but clears the prior result, disclosure receipt, and approval controls. A new external call still needs fresh per-call confirmation. `换个线索` after a complete search starts a blank draft.
- Source-failure notices must work when every source failed as well as when only one failed. Do not call the failure merely `部分来源` or promise that other entrances remain available without evidence; describe each failed source and leave the family's retry/留白 decision open.
- A formal AI source can identify an incomplete response, rejected credentials, rate limit, rejected request shape/address, or invalid opportunity structure through safe reason codes. Today translates those into distinct next steps without displaying provider bodies or promising another source succeeded. An unknown failure stays generic; no partial AI candidate is shown.
- A configured World-information service uses the same safe failure vocabulary for rejected credentials, rate limits, rejected requests, malformed feed, and timeout. Show one actionable explanation for a failed upstream service even if its dependent local World converter also has no records; never repeat a generic failure below a specific one or expose the service response body.
- A successful HTTP reply with a malformed chat envelope is an `入口格式无法读取` problem, not a network failure. The optional fictional-sample check and formal Today result use the same plain-language boundary while never displaying raw provider text.
- On a source-failure result, the mode/retention receipt remains the same neutral inset as a successful result; the detailed source issue owns the single warm caution card. Do not stack two warm warning surfaces for one failure.
- The first visible opportunity starts the decision flow, but route pills and cards carry no ordinal numbers or implied quality ranking. Result mode and retention are concise secondary status lines; “换个线索” is a text action, not a competing filled call-to-action above every entry.
- The route overview names each door, not only its ecosystem, and states whether it mainly serves the child, caregiver, or a shared goal before the family reaches the full cards. Use the same stage-aware goal wording as the cards; do not turn the overview into a fit score or a source-quality claim. Keep each full card focused on the concrete entry, practical limits, goal ownership, verification/source, and any booking, sponsorship, or tracking warning. Put detailed why-now reasoning, age range, dates, and source notes behind an explicit disclosure; they must remain inspectable before or after choosing.
- `verified` from a third-party World Brief or Pack is the publisher's claim, not Jianyu's independent certification. Say who marked it and keep the household's recheck visible. Before opening any original-source HTTPS link, show its destination host and explain that the external site may observe the network visit or track it; cancel must leave the app without opening the link. Reject credential-bearing or malformed destinations.
- An unusually long generated explanation shows at most three lines initially and offers the shared `查看完整说明` / `收起说明` disclosure. The full explanation remains available; only the compact first view changes. Do not apply one-line truncation to the actual entrance title, source, safety warnings, or family decision controls.
- One valid entrance is a complete result, not a failed attempt to fill a list. Page copy uses singular wording when only one actionable entrance survives the local Gate; never call it `几种不同入口` merely because 留白 is also available.
- In the card's compact provenance inset, show verification state and source on separate lines; joining both with a separator can strand `·` or a short provider name at the narrow large-font baseline. Both remain visible before expanding detailed judgment.
- In the route overview, put each ecosystem marker above its full-width entry name. Do not reserve a fixed left column for markers beside potentially long AI/Pack titles; the same stacked pattern applies to 留白 at 360 dp and large font scale.
- Provider-reported confidence is not a family-facing fit score: keep it available to the local Gate and developer evaluation, but do not convert it to a `较贴合` badge. Time, cost, energy, travel, and age are neutral metadata, not amber human-decision status. Opportunity and Nothing use the same neutral card and primary action weight; only their small category markers differ.
- A recent count of selected doors is not proof that any activity happened or that the child liked it. The reference Policy may offer a visible, gentle 留白 reminder, but it must not silently reject a fresh child-led request solely on selection volume or describe selections as completed interventions.
- The Timeline page describes saved choices and evidence, not `真正发生的事` as a blanket claim. Its optional feedback prompt may capture a view before or after trying an entrance, so it must not call the response verified participation or `真实反馈` merely because a choice was tapped.
- Timeline event labels and saved-view explanations follow the same rule: call it a later view, not verified participation, and say approved AI use can treat it as a clue without promising the model will correct or avoid similar entrances.
- In ordinary child-response UI, consistently call it `后来的看法` / `已留下看法`, not alternating with `反馈`; a view can be expressed before or after trying an entrance. Keep `教师反馈` for a separately sourced school record.
- The immediate post-choice acknowledgement uses the shared card-title hierarchy; its optional view label is `我想留个看法` for 13–15 and `代记孩子的看法` for younger stages. Arrange the three response actions with the same wrapping `FlowRow` as the Timeline, so large text can wrap whole buttons rather than compressing their labels.
- Follow-up controls preserve authorship: at 13–15 the child-signed action uses the child callback; at earlier stages a caregiver may explicitly relay a view the child expressed. The UI must not ask for the caregiver's own opinion and then save it as a child response. At 16+ no new child feedback is appended. A v1 feedback without source attribution remains inspectable as `来源未确认` but does not enter later AI context. Both modes state that a tap does not prove participation; shared-device signing is not identity verification.
- Only one optional feedback write may be in flight. Today and Timeline show `正在保存看法…`, disable all response choices and subject/route switching until the local vault write finishes, and keep the current acknowledgement visible on failure. A rapid second tap must not append or overwrite another view.
- Nothing keeps the same card and filled-choice weight, but does not pretend to be an activity with `0 分钟` metadata. Say plainly that choosing it needs no time, cost, or caregiver effort.
- After a 留白 choice, acknowledge the pause without invoking a missed task or lost progress. In 13–15 hand-over, address the young person directly; at younger stages, leave the next interest to the child without prompting routine upkeep.
- A child's explicit veto is durable negative evidence, unlike a caregiver skipping an option. The secondary `孩子不想要` / `我不想要` action requires a plain-language confirmation before writing that evidence; the dialog distinguishes the child's refusal from a caregiver's preference and explains that any later AI use still needs per-call approval.
- After a veto, say that its record may become a clue for a later approved AI request; do not promise the model will never suggest a similar entrance. The follow-up wording still belongs to the actual decision maker: the younger child's refusal is not the caregiver's own veto, while 13–15 addresses the young person directly.
- One discovery may produce only one durable choice, including 留白 or a confirmed veto. While local save is in flight, disable all choice actions and `换个线索`, keep a visible saving label, and show the acknowledgement only after the save succeeds; a rapid second tap must not append a second choice.
- When restricted records exist, the shared timeline keeps the key-holder access caveat visible in the compact notice. A longer explanation of the missing independent identity/key boundary may expand on demand; collapsing it must never imply cryptographic privacy already exists.
- Until independent subject keys and authentication exist, the hand-over retention choice and result receipt must say `不在共享足迹显示`, not `只留给自己` or another child-only secrecy claim. State plainly that holders of the household key or recovery bundle may still read the saved content.
- A failed optional source is a caution, not a destructive error. Preserve a visible explanation without using the red danger surface reserved for deletion or serious risk.
- The discovery mode comes from the family's explicit choice, not from which Provider happened to return candidates. An offline demonstration must say that it stayed on-device; the BYOK receipt describes the AI service's permitted scope. Never present a local processing receipt as if it were a network disclosure receipt.
- Disabled actions must remain visually legible and have nearby copy that makes the missing requirement evident.
- Product UI never displays raw event names, schema fields, provider response bodies, exception messages, paths, or unknown extension codes. Translate known corrective errors and use a stable Chinese fallback for everything else.
- A failed local vault or encrypted-settings write must not reuse the network/file-picker error copy. Name the actual boundary as `本机保存`, keep the form open, and state that the operation did not complete.
- If the final discovery receipt fails to save after a formal source may have been contacted, the error must also say that an external service may already have received the approved context and that retry could send it again. Keep this high-stakes warning in a persistent warm Today card until acknowledged or superseded, not only in an auto-disappearing Snackbar. This warning is not a substitute for a durable pre-send attempt record or a transactional remote receipt.
- Formal discovery saves a category-only approval event in the encrypted Vault before contacting an external source. If that write fails, no source is called. In the Timeline, distinguish `请求前确认的信息范围` from `请求后记录的信息范围`; show the recipient and translated category names in the shared neutral inset used for audit rows, never raw prompt values or protocol IDs. Neither event should claim confirmed network delivery. A request that fails after starting may retain only the first event.
- In formal discovery, a started vault write is not a saved interest or disclosure receipt. On failure, keep the input draft for retry and project only the latest vault-confirmed family state; do not let the Timeline or a later AI context read an optimistic unsaved copy.

## 5. Responsive and accessibility checks

- The baseline acceptance width is 360 dp. Headlines may wrap, but must not strand a short Latin token such as `AI` on its own line.
- Stage-specific Today headlines and primary input labels should be concise enough that a 360 dp / 1.3-font layout does not strand the final one or two Chinese characters. Keep the full nuance in the description and progressive disclosure rather than squeezing the headline to fit with a smaller one-off font.
- Optional School, Life, and World field labels follow that same rule: keep the label short, and place purpose, examples, and sending caveats in supporting text so `（可选）` does not break into an orphaned line at 360 dp / 1.3 font.
- Localized button groups and dynamic choices use `FlowRow`; never pre-split choices into fixed two-column rows.
- Metadata plus a long action label stacks vertically on narrow cards. Do not protect a right-hand action column by squeezing explanatory text into one-character lines.
- Acceptance includes 360 dp at 1.3 font scale in both light and dark themes, not only the default emulator size.
- When one screen replaces a long form with a result or returns from results to a new input, reset the list to its beginning. Never reveal a new decision surface at the previous mode's scroll offset.
- Switching the viewed child is also a new decision surface: reset Today to its heading even when neither child has a discovery result, and clear the old text-field focus so the keyboard does not keep the screen displaced. Keep unsent drafts separately in memory for each child so a return does not silently lose text; never show one child's draft or approval controls under another child. A successful choice or explicit new request clears the relevant old draft, and replacing/erasing the household clears all drafts. During discovery or a choice/view save, pause child switching with a nearby explanation rather than letting an in-flight result appear under another child. A persistent warning that an external request may have been sent must be acknowledged before switching; a child switch may not silently dismiss it.
- Touch targets are at least 48 dp. Decorative icons have no content description; actionable icons do.
- Every release candidate is checked at default font scale and a larger font scale, in light and dark theme, with the accessibility hierarchy inspected.
- Compile, unit tests, and lint are necessary but do not replace emulator/device screenshots and interaction checks.
- Run instrumented UI checks with `scripts/run-android-ui-tests.ps1` on the disposable `Jianyu_UiTests_API_35` AVD. Do not run `connectedDebugAndroidTest` on a daily-use emulator: its cleanup can uninstall the app and erase its local Family Vault. Most isolated UI tests use synthetic in-memory family state; the journey test creates and removes its own synthetic encrypted Vault on that disposable AVD. None opens the daily-use Vault.
- The production discovery-result list is independently renderable in those tests, so synthetic source success and source-failure/fallback can be checked without a model key or Family Vault. Treat that as result-presentation coverage, not proof of a live Provider or the complete composer-to-persistence journey.
- When AI alone suggests a named current event without an original source, the empty result explains the exclusion in short readable lines and still offers 留白. The 360 dp / 1.3-font synthetic result capture is checked in both themes; keep the Nothing card's cost/time note short enough not to strand a final character.
- A separate disposable-AVD journey test drives the actual Today composer through per-call approval, a synthetic AI source, local Gate, visible result, family choice, and encrypted Vault readback. Its fake source intentionally bypasses the live HTTP/model parser; keep parser and real-Provider verification separate from that UI/storage claim.

## 6. Extension boundary

Providers, Policies, Packs, and BrandConfig may add content and branding without bypassing Context Firewall, lifecycle authority, Nothing, local Gate, or capability-status language. A fork changing color, type, icons, or tone should replace tokens/components rather than copy and mutate individual screens.

`tests/ui-system.test.js` enforces the two highest-drift boundaries: product screens may not instantiate raw Material `Card` or `FilterChip` components. Extend the shared components when a real new semantic state is needed.

The Android identity model keeps `taglineZhCN`, `heroZhCN`, and `missionZhCN` separate all the way from the public JSON document to Compose. New clients and forks must preserve those meanings rather than showing one field in every slot. UI tests also reject literal default-brand copy inside product screens, so a fork does not need a risky global text replacement.
