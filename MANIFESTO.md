# Jianyu Manifesto

1. **More doors, fewer prescriptions.** AI expands the visible opportunity space; it does not choose a child's path.
2. **The entry point comes first.** Begin with what the child already wants, not what an adult wants to cover.
3. **Play and life are not disguised worksheets.** An experience must remain worthwhile even when no learning outcome is extracted.
4. **Nothing is a valid recommendation.** Restraint is part of product quality.
5. **Evidence is not identity.** Preserve observations, expressions, uncertainty, contradiction, and change; never freeze a child into a score.
6. **Child, caregiver, and shared goals remain distinct.** AI does not quietly convert a child's interest into a caregiver's curriculum objective.
7. **AI discovers; the Engine constrains; the family chooses; the child validates.** No model has authority to prescribe, enroll, purchase, contact, or define.
8. **Different media open different doors.** Games, stories, sport, repair, family life, nature, travel, making, and the public world belong to one opportunity space.
9. **School and experience are complementary tracks.** Experience forms intuition and questions; formal education names, compresses, corrects, and systematizes knowledge.
10. **The system should become less necessary.** Authority transfers to the child and caregiver-side modeling ends at Graduation.
11. **No daily burden and no completeness requirement.** Sparse data, quiet weeks, and uncertainty are normal.
12. **Your child is not our dataset.** Family data remains under family control, leaves authorized devices only when necessary, and is client-encrypted before synchronization.
13. **Open core, replaceable opinions.** Providers, policies, packs, interfaces, hosting, and educational approaches can change without trapping families.
14. **The reference app has no special magic.** Jianyu consumes the same public FOE interfaces available to everyone else.
15. **Education equity means fairer opportunity, not identical children.** AI should reduce dependence on caregiver education, information channels, and specialist knowledge without promising equal outcomes.
